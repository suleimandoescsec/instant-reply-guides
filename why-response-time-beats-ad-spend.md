# Why response time beats ad spend for small businesses

Small businesses often buy more traffic when sales slow down. The cheaper fix is usually hiding in the inbox: answering faster.

## The math is simple

A customer who messages three shops buys from the one that answers first, most of the time. When a business replies in seconds instead of hours, more of its existing conversations turn into orders, with no extra money spent on ads.

Paid ads bring people to the door. Response time decides whether they come in.

## What slow replies actually cost

Every unanswered DM is a small leak. One or two a day feels harmless, but across a month it adds up to real revenue, especially for businesses where a single order covers the cost of a whole month of tooling.
