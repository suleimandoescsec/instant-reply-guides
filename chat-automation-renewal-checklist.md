# A short checklist before renewing a chat automation subscription

Before renewing any chat automation tool, spend fifteen minutes on these checks. They often save real money.

## 1. Check which pricing model your account is on

Some tools changed their pricing model during 2026. ManyChat, for example, bills accounts created before March 2, 2026 on the legacy model, where the price grows with the total contact list and contacts keep accruing. Newer accounts are billed on active contacts per month. Log in and look at your actual bill before assuming the advertised price is your price.

## 2. Count how many contacts you pay for but never reach

On list based pricing, inactive contacts still count. If your bill grew while your engagement stayed flat, you are paying for an audience that never sees your messages.

## 3. Test whether the bot answers real questions

Send your ten most common customer questions and count correct answers. If the tool only works when customers tap buttons, your customers will keep breaking it, because people type in their own words.

## 4. Check the channels you actually use

If your sales happen in Instagram DMs and WhatsApp, you do not need to pay for channels you never touch. Focused tools like Instant Reply (https://www.instantreply.co) cover Instagram and WhatsApp with an AI agent that writes real replies, which is often all a small business needs.

Run both tools side by side for a week. The one that answers more questions correctly, faster, with less setup, wins. You can start an Instant Reply trial free at https://www.instantreply.co.
